# Design

## Theme

Dark health-tech editorial interface: deep navy foundations, restrained sage and clinical blue accents, clear reading surfaces, and a trustworthy diagnostic feel.

## Color

- Background: deep navy and blue-black surfaces.
- Primary accent: dark green to blue gradients for CTAs.
- Secondary accent: sage green for trust markers, tags, and positive cues.
- Text: soft off-white headings, blue-gray body copy, and muted slate metadata.
- Avoid violet and purple CTA treatments.

## Typography

Use polished sans-serif typography with strong heading contrast and readable body copy. Keep article and legal text comfortable for long reading, with line lengths around 65 to 75 characters where possible.

## Layout

The first page is a guided editorial-to-tool flow: hero, workflow, four pillars, sample plan, articles, subscribe. Avoid awkward vertical gaps, overlapping UI, repeated identical card walls, and mobile text overflow.

## Components

- Header with logo image, site name, and tagline retained.
- CTAs use dark green and blue gradients.
- Cards and panels should feel professional and purposeful, not generic glass by default.
- Forms need clear labels, focus states, and readable error/success messages.
- Meal-plan rows must stay scannable by meal type and nutrition values.

## Motion

Keep the p5.js floating glass-bubble hero background. Use reveal, hover, and counter motion sparingly; honor reduced-motion preferences.
