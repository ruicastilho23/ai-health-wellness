# Full-Frame Panoramic Dr. Ava Chen Design

## Goal

Make every Dr. Ava Chen appearance a native full-frame 16:9 panoramic studio shot. Never crop the presenter into a narrow side panel, split screen, portrait insert, circular cutout, or partial-frame window.

## Reference

Use `https://youtu.be/cbkGJykTRGY` as the visual reference for presenter scale, panoramic framing, negative space, and studio atmosphere.

## Framing Contract

- Apply this rule to all four avatar appearances: `AVA-01`, `AVA-02`, `AVA-03`, and `AVA-04`.
- Generate or export every avatar clip natively at 1920 x 1080, 16:9.
- Fill the complete video rectangle with the avatar scene and its environment; do not place a smaller avatar video inside another canvas.
- Position Ava slightly right of center, as in the reference, with useful negative space on the left for short editorial text or motion-card overlays.
- Keep Ava comfortably inside the frame. Her head, shoulders, torso, arms, hands when visible, and intended lower-body framing must not be unintentionally clipped by the left, right, top, or bottom edges.
- Use a medium-wide or mid-thigh composition similar to the reference rather than an oversized talking-head crop.
- Do not zoom, crop, or reframe the exported avatar clip into a side-panel layout during assembly or CapCut finishing.

## Background Contract

Create a background closely matching the reference's mood without pixel-copying it:

- Full-width dark charcoal or deep teal health-tech studio.
- Cool blue illuminated vertical fins or light bars on the left.
- Warm amber practical shelving lights on the right.
- Subtle shelves, monitors, or a restrained console for depth.
- Cinematic contrast with clear separation between Ava and the background.
- Clean, credible, professional atmosphere; no generic hospital stock room, virtual newsroom, neon cyberpunk overload, or distracting objects.

The background must remain consistent across all four appearances unless a deliberate, approved variation preserves the same studio identity.

## Presenter-Adjacent Graphics

Presenter-adjacent information, evidence, comparison, source, or statistic cards must use the mandatory HyperFrames motion-card kit. Place them in available negative space or animate them over the full-frame studio composition without shrinking, cropping, masking, or pushing Ava into a narrow panel.

## Quality Gates

For every avatar clip, inspect start, middle, and end frames and reject the clip when:

- The canvas is not native 16:9.
- Ava is cropped by a side-panel or split-screen treatment.
- Any intended body area is unintentionally cut off.
- Ava is oversized, centered like a conventional talking head, or positioned without useful negative space.
- The background does not fill the full rectangle.
- The studio direction materially departs from the blue-left, warm-right reference atmosphere.
- Presenter-adjacent graphics obscure Ava's face, hands, or torso.

Regenerate a failed clip; never hide the framing problem beneath B-roll or crop it during editing.
