# Longevity Escape Velocity Cinematic Revision Design

## Objective

Revise the approved seven-minute YouTube video without changing its narrative structure, presenter duration, or core content. The revision replaces the flat visual treatment with the approved cinematic studio atmosphere, upgrades the infographic system, and creates a stable continuous audio mix.

## Locked Requirements

- 1920x1080, 16:9, full-screen YouTube presentation.
- Keep the presenter fully visible and intact. Do not crop her into a strip, card, or split screen.
- Use the approved presenter preview as the visual target: dark studio room, cool blue edge light on the left, warm practical light on the right, and visible but restrained room depth.
- No people or distracting movement in the background.
- Never place graphics over the presenter.
- No subtitles, captions, karaoke text, or spoken-word transcription.
- Preserve the existing approximately seven-minute duration and the current short HeyGen presenter allocation. Do not spend additional HeyGen credits unless the existing source proves unusable.

## Approaches Considered

1. **Global cosmetic grade only:** Fast, but it cannot create believable room depth or repair the basic card system.
2. **Regenerate all scenes:** Maximum freedom, but it adds cost and risks changing the approved presenter and timing.
3. **Targeted compositing and motion rebuild:** Preserve the existing presenter and narration, replace the environment, rebuild the graphics, and remix audio. This is the selected approach because it delivers the requested quality without additional avatar generation.

## Presenter Scenes

The three presenter anchors retain their current timing and full-screen framing. The flat navy background is replaced with a cinematic room plate matching the approved preview. The room remains visually quiet: soft shelving, dark charcoal materials, subtle blue vertical illumination, and a small warm practical light.

The presenter remains centered with her head, shoulders, hands, and lower torso visible. Editorial topic overlays occupy the left negative space and small lower-right information zone. Overlays use slim borders, restrained translucency, crisp sans serif typography, and teal accents. They do not resemble subtitles and do not track every spoken phrase.

## Graphic Scenes

The existing basic full-width cards are replaced with an editorial medical-technology motion system:

- Layered translucent surfaces instead of repeated opaque boxes.
- Strong visual hierarchy with one primary idea per scene.
- Data-driven diagrams, evidence ladders, biomarker signals, and systems maps.
- Controlled teal, cool blue, white, and small warm amber accents.
- Subtle depth through localized light, fine grid structure, and soft shadows.
- Directional reveals, line drawing, number progression, and staggered entrances.
- Calm continuous ambient motion; no random floating objects or decorative people.
- Purposeful transitions that carry a line, mask, or light field between scenes instead of abrupt cuts.

Each scene keeps safe margins and readable typography at 1080p. Cards and labels must not overlap, clip, or exceed their containers.

## Audio Design

The current master measures approximately -17.1 LUFS integrated with an 11.5 LU loudness range. The large range and audible boundary changes come from separately fading every segment to silence and concatenating the results.

The revised mix will:

- Build dialogue as one continuous timeline rather than isolated scene files.
- Match presenter and narration sections before assembly.
- Use short equal-power crossfades at edit boundaries so words and room tone do not snap.
- Apply gentle dialogue compression and peak control after assembly.
- Normalize the final master to approximately -14 LUFS integrated with true peak no higher than -1 dBTP.
- Preserve natural speech dynamics and avoid pumping or aggressive noise reduction.

No music will be added unless an existing approved music bed is already part of the source. Any retained bed must remain consistently below dialogue.

## Deliverables

- Revised full-resolution YouTube master.
- Upload-ready copy in the existing CapCut import/output folder.
- Representative presenter and infographic review frames.
- Audio verification report including integrated loudness, loudness range, and true peak.

## Acceptance Checks

- Presenter is not cropped and is never covered by graphics.
- Background matches the approved cinematic preview throughout presenter scenes.
- Graphic scenes use the upgraded visual language and contain no accidental overlaps.
- No subtitle or caption stream exists.
- Scene changes have visible transition treatment and no abrupt audio cuts.
- Full video decodes successfully at 1920x1080 with stereo audio.
- Final loudness is close to -14 LUFS and true peak is at or below -1 dBTP.
