# AI Health Avatar Long-Form Video Skill Design

## Objective

Create a separate reusable skill for 7–8 minute AI Health & Wellness Hub YouTube videos that preserves the approved no-avatar production workflow while adding four mandatory Dr. Ava Shen avatar appearances generated through the existing HeyGen account.

## Relationship to the Existing Skill

The new skill inherits the complete quality contract from `ai-health-longform-multitool-video`: authoritative research, claim tracking, a strong first-15-second hook, 45–55 micro-scenes, 20–26 unique generated image plates, official HyperFrames catalog components, one or two detailed Blender animations, female brand-voice continuity, FFmpeg assembly, real CapCut finishing, and final artifact validation.

The new skill remains separate so an avatar request cannot accidentally alter or trigger the no-avatar workflow.

## Avatar Structure

Use exactly four Dr. Ava Shen appearances totaling a target of 110–115 seconds and an absolute maximum of 120 seconds. Avatar time replaces narration time and must not extend the final runtime.

1. Opening hook: 30–35 seconds, full-frame and visually strong.
2. First explanation checkpoint: 25–30 seconds.
3. Evidence and limitations checkpoint: 25–30 seconds.
4. Final takeaway and safety note: 25–30 seconds.

Use full-frame layouts for the opening and closing appearances. Use professionally composed side-panel layouts for the two middle appearances. Each entrance and exit must connect to purposeful HyperFrames motion, sourced data, generated imagery, or Blender animation.

## Avatar Production Contract

Mark four exact `AVA` script segments before asset production. Generate their audio with the same adult female Dr. Ava Shen brand voice used for non-avatar narration, then use those clips with the existing Dr. Ava Shen HeyGen avatar. Do not enable automatic subtitles.

Every avatar clip must provide:

- The correct existing Dr. Ava Shen avatar.
- 1080p output.
- Natural delivery and accurate lip-sync.
- Clean, professional framing with composition space where required.
- The approved continuous female voice identity.
- Fresh current-project output and provenance logging.
- No duplicated lines or burned-in subtitles.

If a mandatory clip has poor lip-sync, unnatural delivery, incorrect framing, visible artifacts, the wrong voice, subtitles, or another quality failure, regenerate it until it passes. Never exclude it, shorten it away, conceal it beneath graphics, or replace it with narration-only footage. All four appearances are required in the final video.

If HeyGen cannot accept the continuity-preserving audio workflow, stop with the concrete blocker rather than silently substituting a different voice.

## Visual and Editorial Rules

Avatar footage is a narrative anchor, not static padding. Documentary sections retain the same pace, diversity, and premium visual standards as the approved no-avatar workflow. The clean master contains no automated subtitles, transcript captions, karaoke text, or sentence-by-sentence narration captions. Short editorial titles, figures, chart labels, sources, safety notes, and calls to action remain permitted.

The validator must enforce avatar count, per-clip evidence, combined duration, the 120-second ceiling, voice continuity, resolution, subtitle absence, regeneration evidence for failed clips, unique imagery, HyperFrames distribution, Blender evidence, runtime, audio, CapCut provenance, and final visual review.

## First Production

Topic: AI is designing new weapons against antibiotic resistance.

Core promise: Explain how AI can explore antimicrobial-peptide possibilities faster and prioritize experimental candidates that traditional searches might miss.

The four Dr. Ava Shen segments cover:

1. Hook: AI may help rebuild medicine's shrinking antibiotic arsenal.
2. Explanation: how AI-designed antimicrobial peptides are proposed and prioritized.
3. Evidence: what laboratory testing can establish and where the evidence stops.
4. Safety and takeaway: these are experimental candidates, not approved antibiotics, treatments, or demonstrated human cures.

Primary source: `https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/ai-tool-could-speed-antibiotic-development`

Visual seed: a bright molecular laboratory with clean peptide structures converging on a resistant bacterium in optimistic green-blue scientific light.

Keywords: antibiotic discovery, antimicrobial resistance, AI drug design, MedicalAI, DrugDiscovery.

## Failure Handling

Fail closed when the required avatar, matching audio workflow, official HyperFrames components, image providers, Blender production, CapCut export, or required validation evidence cannot be produced. Do not downgrade a failed mandatory component into filler or remove an avatar appearance.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A distinct discoverable avatar-video skill and reusable launcher prompt exist.
- The original no-avatar skill remains unchanged in meaning and triggering.
- The new validator rejects missing avatar clips, fewer or more than four appearances, total avatar time over 120 seconds, failed clip quality, voice mismatch, burned-in subtitles, or missing HeyGen provenance.
- The first antibiotic-resistance project follows the new workflow and produces a verified final CapCut export.
