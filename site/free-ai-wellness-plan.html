A good wellness plan starts small. Use the free meal generator for nutrition, then add one movement habit, one sleep habit, and one daily check-in.

Your first week

  • Generate a 7-day meal plan and choose two repeatable meals.
  • Walk 20 minutes on at least four days.
  • Set a consistent wake time.
  • Write down energy, hunger, and sleep quality once per day.

After seven days, keep what worked and change only the parts that felt unrealistic.
