7-day high-protein meal plan for fat loss

A fat-loss meal plan works best when it is repeatable. High-protein meals can make the week easier by improving fullness and protecting lean mass while calories come down.

How to use the generator

  1. Enter your current weight.
  2. Select fat loss as your primary goal.
  3. Choose the high-protein meal style.
  4. Use the result as a starting point and adjust portions based on hunger, training, and professional advice.

Simple weekly structure

Keep breakfast protein-forward, make lunch easy to pack, use a planned snack, and keep dinner built around lean protein plus vegetables and a controlled carbohydrate portion.
