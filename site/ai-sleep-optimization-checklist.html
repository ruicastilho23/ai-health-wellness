Recovery checklist

AI sleep optimization checklist

Sleep affects hunger, recovery, and consistency. Use this checklist with the meal generator so your nutrition plan has a better chance of surviving real life.

7-day checklist

  • Set one consistent wake time for the week.
  • Stop caffeine 8 hours before bed.
  • Keep dinner satisfying but not oversized.
  • Review your sleep tracker for patterns, not perfection.
  • Plan tomorrow's breakfast before you go to sleep.

AI sleep tools are useful when they help you notice patterns and make one practical change at a time.
