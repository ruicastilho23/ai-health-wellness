# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Health-curious readers and self-improvement beginners who want a trustworthy, accessible entry point into AI-supported nutrition, fitness, mental wellness, and sleep. They may arrive from search or social links, scan articles, compare tools, and use the meal generator as a lightweight first action.

## Product Purpose

AI Health & Wellness Hub exists to turn AI wellness topics into clear guidance, preserved long-form articles, affiliate-supported recommendations, and a practical 7-day meal planning experience. Success means visitors trust the site, understand the four wellness pillars, subscribe for the generator, and can navigate the content without confusion or visual fatigue.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, calm. The brand should feel like a health-tech editorial hub with practical tools: professional and reassuring, but still modern enough to feel AI-native.

## Anti-references

Avoid cartoon wellness, loud neon fitness branding, generic purple AI SaaS visuals, cluttered affiliate-blog layouts, exaggerated glassmorphism, and anything that makes medical or nutritional claims feel unserious. Do not remove or rewrite preserved article content, legal links, affiliate links, disclaimers, or the meal generator.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with trust before excitement.
2. Make the path from education to action obvious.
3. Treat long-form content as a first-class product surface.
4. Keep the meal generator integrated, not bolted on.
5. Use motion to support orientation, never spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, readable text sizes, keyboard-friendly navigation, careful mobile spacing, and reduced-motion support. Color cannot be the only signal for form feedback or status.
